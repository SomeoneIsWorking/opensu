Console drawings for the systems in `src/library/rom_systems.cpp`: `<system>.svg` is the picture on a
console's card in Library, `glyphs/<system>.svg` the small glyph in a framed tile's tab. Both are
named by ES-DE system name and drawn white on a 64 x 64 box; openSU tints them and puts them on the
platform's colours.

They are openSU's own drawings of the hardware, under the repository's MIT licence (`LICENSE`).
They carry no company's logo, wordmark or box art.
